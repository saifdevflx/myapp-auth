Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Universal Social Sharing ("the App") handles your information.

1. Information We Access

When you connect a social media account (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, etc.), the App requests only the permissions needed to perform the sharing features you use, such as:

  • Basic profile information (e.g. name, account ID)
  • Permission to publish content on your behalf, only when you initiate a post

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not access, read, or store your private messages, contacts, or browsing history on any connected platform. The App does not store your passwords for any social media platform; authentication is handled directly by each platform's official login system.

3. How We Use Access Tokens

Access tokens obtained after you log in to a platform are stored securely on your own device and are used only to perform actions you explicitly request within the App (such as publishing a post). Tokens are not shared with third parties.

4. Data Storage

The App does not operate a central server that stores your personal data. Authentication tokens are kept locally on your device using secure storage.

5. Third-Party Platforms

Each connected platform (Meta, TikTok, etc.) has its own privacy policy governing how it handles your data once you authorize the App. We encourage you to review those policies as well.

6. Data Deletion

You can disconnect any connected account at any time from within the App, which removes the locally stored access token. You can also revoke the App's access directly from each platform's own account settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the app's official support channel.
